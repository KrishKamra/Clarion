Product photos
==============
Drop a photo for a product here and the app shows it automatically,
in the Fit Stream, Radar, Compare header and the product page.

Name the file after the product id, as .jpg, .png or .webp
(square or 4:3 works best, ~800 px wide, light or neutral background):

  bp_atlas.jpg        bp_metro.jpg        hp_pro.jpg
  hp_core.jpg         sling_field.jpg     lamp_halo.jpg
  org_kova.jpg        kb_loom.jpg         mouse_glide.jpg
  mouse_ergo.jpg      bottle_thermo.jpg   notebook_dot.jpg
  pb_volt.jpg         stand_lift.jpg      earb_pulse.jpg
  cam_clear.jpg       cushion_lumbar.jpg

Products without a photo keep their icon on the studio stage.
After adding files run `flutter pub get` (or a full restart) once.
